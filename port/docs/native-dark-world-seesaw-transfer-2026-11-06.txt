Dark World: traversing the two native seesaws

Both seesaws use the original KM1_SEESAW profile and their original
model, collision, tilt and rider behavior. The opt-in
SM64DS_PC_TEST_DARK_WORLD_SEESAW_TRANSFER replay continues the regular
seesaw test after Mario boards the lower plank. It uses the raised end
of that plank to jump onto the second and continues across it.

The jump off the second plank can reach the upper stone platform with
the original ledge grab. Holding forward through the climb moves Mario
clear of the lip before releasing input. The replay recognizes supported
landings on either stone surface and makes no changes to gameplay rules,
actor placement, player position or save data.

Scope: Mario crossing these two seesaws in Dark World. The nearby
floor switch, moving stairs traversal, and boss entry are separate tests.
