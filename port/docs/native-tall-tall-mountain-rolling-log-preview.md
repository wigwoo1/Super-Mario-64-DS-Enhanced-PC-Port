# Tall Tall Mountain rolling log preview

The native port registers Tall Tall Mountain’s rolling log using the same movement and collision code as the Lethal Lava Land log, with the mountain log’s own resources and travel limit.

The native actor-data audit passed. A muted replay on a fresh ROM-free kit reconstruction loaded an earned 38-Star save, entered Mission 1 through the original painting and mission selector, and confirmed that the log spawned.

Separate muted development replays used controller inputs to climb from the course entrance and walk onto the log. The collision trace confirmed that the moving log supported Mario while he walked its length. The jump in that replay missed the far bank.

Another muted replay moved the log through normal play, used the original Exit Course menu, and re-entered Mission 1 through the castle painting. A fresh log spawned at the original position, Mario regained control, and both isolated save files stayed byte-identical.

A later muted controller replay entered Mission 2, Mystery of the Monkey Cage, through the original painting and mission selector. The moving log supported Mario, and he jumped to the far bank and reached the summit. He grabbed Ukiki, followed him to the cage, and collected the Star dropped near the waterfall. The original Save & Continue advanced the isolated File A from 39 to 40 Stars and returned control in the castle. A fresh process then loaded that save, selected Mission 2 again and repeated the full route with controller inputs. Picking up the released Star kept the total at 40. Save & Continue returned Mario to the castle, and both isolated save files matched their pre-replay bytes.

In a muted development replay of Mission 3, Scary 'Shrooms, Red Coins, Mario collected all eight red coins and descended to the reward mushroom. Collecting the Power Star and choosing Save & Continue increased the isolated File A total from 40 to 41 and recorded 16 coins. A fresh process loaded that save, selected the same mission, and repeated the route. Mario collected the previously earned Star and returned to the castle through Save & Continue with 41 Stars. Both isolated save files stayed byte-identical on the repeat.

A muted Mission 4 replay verified the original entrance, the full hidden slide and the exit back to the mountain. Mario steered through the far turn, crossed the broken sections and regained control on the reward terrace. A subsequent controller replay repeated the full slide and jumped into the Mission 4 Power Star on the terrace. The original Save & Continue raised the isolated File A total from 41 to 42 Stars, saved a 41-coin course record and returned control in the castle.

A fresh process reloaded the earned 42-Star File A and replayed Mission 4 through the original selector, hidden slide and terrace. The previously earned Star accepted contact again. The total stayed at 42 and both isolated save files were byte-identical after Save & Continue.

In a muted Mission 5 replay, Blast to the Lonely Mushroom, Mario opened the cannon with the Bob-omb Buddy, used the mushroom warp and followed the lower ledge to the cannon. He collected the Power Star during the shot and landed on the mushroom. Save & Continue recorded 43 Stars and the cannon unlock in the isolated File A. A fresh process selected Mission 5 again. The cannon started open, Mario repeated the pickup and the total stayed at 43. Both isolated save files matched their pre-replay bytes after Save & Continue.
