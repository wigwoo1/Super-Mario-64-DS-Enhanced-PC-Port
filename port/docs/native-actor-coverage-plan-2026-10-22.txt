# Complete native actor and level-object coverage

Requested 2026-10-22. Status: PLANNED; missing-object reports are not yet
reproduced or fixed. This milestone remains part of the full PC-port goal.

Player report: bridges, metal gates, buttons/switches and other objects are
missing in some levels. Audit every course, castle area, sub-area and minigame
for the actors and scenery expected in the original game. Cover enemies,
platforms, bridges, doors, gates, switches, interactive objects, collectibles,
effects, and objects created dynamically by other actors or scripts.

1. Build a coverage inventory from the user's local ROM scene/object records
   and the native actor registry. Track course, area, mission, actor type and
   variant, native implementation/resource status, and the original conditions
   under which it should exist. Keep ROM-derived scene inventories private.
   Treat missing static geometry, textures or render submissions separately
   when an apparently missing object is not an actor-spawn failure.
2. Record unsupported profiles, missing factories/callbacks, unavailable
   overlays/resources, initialization failures and failed dynamic spawns with
   enough scene/actor context to reproduce them. Do not silently discard an
   actor or count a placeholder as implemented. Distinguish absent actors from
   actors that exist but are invisible, culled, misplaced or missing collision.
3. Prioritize bridges, metal gates and switches because they can block access
   and progression. Integrate their original behavior through the existing
   native closure and resource system, preserving dependencies between actors.
   Preserve original mission, character, Star, save and event spawn conditions;
   do not spawn every conditional object simultaneously to satisfy coverage.
4. Verify each integration with a native build and relevant profile/data,
   adapter and behavior checks. In muted runtime tests with isolated QA saves,
   demonstrate visible objects, correct placement, collision, interaction,
   animation and linked effects (for example a switch opening its gate).
   Test dynamic creation/destruction, area transitions and course re-entry.
   Check saving/reloading and reward credit where those actors affect progress.
5. Maintain per-course/area/mission coverage and unresolved entries, including
   dynamically created actors and minigames. Record exact build and replay
   evidence, and show screenshots in the chat. Registry entries or successful
   draw callbacks alone do not prove that an object appears and works in play.

Acceptance: all expected actor types/variants and required scenery have native
support, original conditional behavior is retained, and level/mission coverage
demonstrates appearance and gameplay function. Unchecked content stays marked
unverified; no claim that all actors spawn based only on one course or mission.

Preserve the original ROM and user saves. Full executables, extracted assets,
scene inventories, private traces and screenshots remain local under the
existing publication rules. Refresh ROM-free build support only through the
reviewed reconstruction and publication workflow when source closure grows.
