Root cause and repair
The original Player has two head-contact paths. The short head ray in
func_ov002_020cef84 stops upward motion and sets Player collision bit8 before
its larger sphere ever records a ceiling hit. The centered diagnostic showed
sphere flags0x20 and Player flags0x08 at6806; therefore the sphere ceiling
helper correctly reported no hit. The short ray had found the correct block,
but its recovered raw slot28 call used a free-function signature. On native
x86 that passes the object on the stack instead of the ECX register expected
by the C++ method. The host Player transform now calls the same virtual method
through dActor_c::OnHitFromUnderneath with the original owner and attacker.
No contact, character, water, distance or reward eligibility guard is removed.
No forced hit, altered physics, direct pilot reward call or pose edit is used.
