Crash cause and repair
Four recovered player music helpers declare EndMusic(unsigned char) and make
six one-argument calls, while the actual linked function is
Sound::EndMusic(unsigned int, unsigned int). Its second argument is unused by
the retail implementation. Under the native x86 optimizer, EndMusic reuses
that argument's stack slot to tail-call the background music restart. With
only one argument supplied, this writes over a caller's saved register.
In the saved failure, the Feather pointer in EBX became58, the background
music track being restored, then Feather::Behavior faulted immediately after
returning from InitWingFeathers. The exact saved executable/map and its
disassembly confirm the fault is in Feather::Behavior, not GPU retirement.

The generated host Player source now declares the complete void-returning
two-argument function and passes zero in its unused second slot at all six
sites. player_music_host.py makes inventory-checked substitutions. Disassembly
of the repaired executable confirms the explicit second push and eight-byte
caller cleanup. No sound sequence, actor eligibility, reward, or physics rule
is changed. CMake tracks both new host adapter modules for regeneration.
