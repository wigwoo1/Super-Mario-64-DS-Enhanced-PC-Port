Bowser in the Sky development checkpoint

The native build restores selected extending platform, seesaw, rotating disc,
wheel, Whomp and timed-stair actor behavior. A muted replay starts from a
prepared 80-Star File A, enters through the original castle route, defeats
Whomp and Chuckya, activates the floor switch, climbs all six treads and ends
on the upper landing after the stairs reset. This checks the named route and
does not establish earned prerequisites, lift traversal, the final Bowser
fight, credits or campaign completion. Alternate exploratory routes in
the opt-in replay code are diagnostic unless individually verified.

The build kit regenerates the Whomp and timed-stair translation units from
the user's matching ROM. The public source and kit contain the adapter code
and symbol names. The full executable is produced locally.

Final arena and ending checkpoint

A selected muted replay from the original castle entrance uses a prepared
80-Star File A. Mario crosses the upper moving platforms, hits Bowser with
three mines, collects the giant Star and reaches the credits and final cake
card. The original confirmation returns to the main menu. A stale dialogue
card remains over the returned menu, and menu controls after the credits
remain unverified. The two isolated save files were unchanged in that run.
This checkpoint does not establish earned campaign progression, ending save
persistence or coverage of the remaining courses and requested features.

The ending changes restore the giant Star actor, extend the player animation
table through the last ending animation, fix the native particle callback,
and restore ending sprite and layout references. Credits use two original
panels side by side with the gameplay counters and minimap hidden. The
matching ROM supplies the animation and sprite data during local assembly.
