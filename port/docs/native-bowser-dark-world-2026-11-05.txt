Native Bowser and Dark World: selected diagnostic checks, unfinished game

Native Bowser, his tail, fire, spike bombs and key remain behind the
SM64DS_PC_BOWSER_PREVIEW environment option. Selected isolated tests verify
Mario grabbing and spinning the tail, an ordinary throw into a native bomb,
defeat, key pickup, original Save & Continue and restored castle control.
A fresh-process repeat verifies another key pickup without duplicate saved
credit. The saved key opens the original basement door; saving and reloading
preserves that door's unlocked flag. Fire with Mario unlocked drops his cap;
an empty eligible-character mask uses the existing coin branch instead of
entering an unbounded cap-selection loop.

Six native diamond platforms (KM1_UKISHIMA, actor 135) now spawn in course 35.
Their original square paths, resources and velocity-based rider collision are
connected. An ordinary-input test enters through the original castle's
12-star Mario gate and trapdoor, dismisses the course intro, traverses the
opening path, activates the timed switch, lands on its temporary brick and
returns safely before expiry. Mario then boards a moving diamond, rides a
full cycle and jumps onto the far bank. The final selected muted run has 504
grounded static-floor samples after that landing, no deaths, no audio errors
and unchanged isolated save files. Six focused input/session/camera tests pass.

not yet been accepted; do not describe the complete wheel as playable.
